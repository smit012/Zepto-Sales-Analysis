# Zepto-Sales-Analysis
PowerBI, Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
Zepto is a leading quick commerce platform, enabling rapid grocery and essential deliveries. This **Power BI dashboard** visualizes Zepto's transaction data, providing insights into **sales trends, order distribution, brand performance, and category-wise sales**.

## 🎯 Goals
- Develop a **dynamic dashboard** to track:
  - 💰 **Total Sales**: ₹18M in revenue
  - 📦 **Total Quantity**: 30K products sold
  - 🛒 **Total Orders**: 10K unique order transactions
  - 🌍 **Regional Performance**: Orders by location
  - 🏪 **Store-wise Analysis**: Top-performing Zepto hubs
  - 📦 **Category-Wise Orders & Sales**: Popular product categories
  - 🕒 **Delivery Performance**: On-time vs. delayed orders
  - 🏷️ **Top Brands & Products**: Most ordered brands and items
- Enable **interactive filtering** for deeper analysis
- Provide **data-driven insights** for business strategy

## 👥 Target Audience
- **Business Analysts**: Sales trends and performance metrics
- **Supply Chain Teams**: Inventory and delivery performance
- **Marketing & Product Managers**: Popular brands & categories

## 🚀 Key Features
- **📈 Intuitive Visuals**: Bar charts, pie charts, and KPIs for better insights
- **📊 Order Trends**:
  - 🏙 **Top Locations**: Cities with the highest order volume
  - 🏪 **Store Performance**: Best-performing Zepto hubs
  - 📦 **Product Demand**: Most frequently purchased items
- **⏳ Delivery Performance**:
  - ✅ **On-Time Deliveries**: 20.1% orders delivered as scheduled
  - ❌ **Delayed Deliveries**: 79.9% of orders facing delays
- **🛒 Category-Wise Sales**:
  - 📊 **Grocery** (32.21%), **Personal Care** (27.51%), and **Beverages** (22.06%) drive sales
  - 🏷️ **Top Brands**: Dabur (34.15%), Cadbury (23.02%), Amul (17.25%)
- **📂 Export & Sharing**: Easily share insights with stakeholders

## 📌 Expected Outcomes
✅ Identify sales trends and high-performing products  
✅ Improve delivery performance and reduce delays  
✅ Understand regional demand for better inventory planning
